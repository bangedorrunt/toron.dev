That last pair of assertions exists because the first build of it was wrong: awaiting
requestAnimationFrame inside the transition callback deadlocks (rendering, and with it
every frame callback, is held while the callback is pending). Measured in isolation:
frame wait -> frames 1 to 2 and the callback never completed, ready never settled;
timer wait -> frames 0 to 71 and finished. Written the frame way the morph never
played and every rAF animation on the page stayed frozen behind it. The settle signal
is now the router's own pathname plus a 1.5s bail-out.
